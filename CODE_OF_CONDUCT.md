# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Classroom Desk welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We aim to keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and focused on helping students and educators.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful.
- Discuss ideas and code in good faith; criticize work, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback and make room for others to participate.
- Protect privacy. Do not post passwords, OAuth tokens, student records, private course content, or other personal information.
- Follow the project's security reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement and reporting

The project maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep project spaces safe and constructive. To report a concern, email [jarvis404.dev@gmail.com](mailto:jarvis404.dev@gmail.com) with relevant links and a concise description. Do not include credentials or private Classroom data. The maintainer will review reports and decide on an appropriate response.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, and other spaces operated by the Classroom Desk project.
